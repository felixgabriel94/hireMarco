# Problems the master does not fix: send them to the mix

**What to look for.** Clicks or dropouts, clipped samples, a DC offset,
silence at the head or the tail, a mix already limited hard, a mix with
little dynamic movement.

**Threshold.** Any of these findings.

**Why.** Mastering is not a remix (R-AUD-0005) and works on the finished
mix (R-AUD-0004); a click, a clip or a dead tail is fixed in the mix or
the bounce range, and a mix that arrives limited leaves nothing to master
(master rulebook, Merrill line).

**When it applies.** Always: the finding goes in the report as a note to
the mix with the number, and the master proceeds only where the problem
does not poison the result (a clip is reported and the master still
prints; a limited-hard mix stops and asks).

**When it does not.** The artist says the demo ships as is: then the
master prints with the note attached.

**The move.** None on the master. Clicks: the mix's click repair per take;
tail silence: the bounce range ends at the song's last marker; DC offset:
a high-pass on the offending track in the mix; limited hard: ask for the
mix with headroom.

**Expected effect.** The note in the record and the caption; the next mix
print without the finding.

**Verify.** The next audit.
