# A resonance: one frequency standing over its octave

**What to look for.** The audit's resonance finding: a narrow peak in the
long-term spectrum that stands a measured number of dB over the octave
around it, named with its frequency; the note it falls on and whether that
note belongs to the song's key.

**Threshold.** The audit reports a resonance at 4 dB over its octave or more;
the plan treats one at 6 dB or more as a problem to remove and one between
4 and 6 as a judgment call made against the key (a ring on a chord tone can
be tone; a ring off the key is a problem).

**Why.** A ring at one frequency is heard as boxiness, honk or a whistle on
every playback system, and a compressor fed a ring pumps on it (master
rulebook, order step 2). Mastering removes trouble with gentle changes
(R-AUD-0005), so the cut is as narrow and as small as the number allows.

**When it applies.** The ring is in the whole song (long-term), not one
section. The mix cannot be reopened, or the artist asked for the master.

**When it does not.** The ring belongs to one take or one section: that is a
mixing move on the take (dynamic band or soothe2), and the note goes to the
mix. The peak is the fundamental of the bass or kick on a chord tone and the
genre wants it: then it is tone, and the plan says so.

**The move.** Pro-Q 3, one band, Bell (or Notch when the ring is pure and
the cut must be total): frequency at the measured peak, typed as the note
when the peak is a note (R-AUD-0007); gain between -1 and -4 dB, scaled to
the measured excess (a 6 dB excess does not take a 6 dB cut; half of the
excess is the starting reason); Q between 4 and 12, narrower for a purer
ring. When the ring comes and goes, the same band made dynamic with a
negative dynamic range of the same size and the auto threshold (R-AUD-0006),
so the cut moves only when the ring does.

**Expected effect.** The print's resonance list no longer carries that
frequency, or carries it under 4 dB over its octave; the band around it is
within 0.5 dB of the mix; tilt unchanged.

**Verify.** The audit of the print against the mix (verify.compare): the
resonance gone, the band delta within the plan's move, no new modulation at
the beat or the bar; the bench shows the insert earned its place.
