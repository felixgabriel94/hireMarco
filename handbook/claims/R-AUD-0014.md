# soothe2's depth, sharpness and selectivity: how much, how narrow, and how choosy the resonance suppression is

## Claim

In soothe2, depth is the sensitivity or threshold of the processing (more depth, more reduction; set to the input even with presets; the number is not dB), sharpness sets how deep and narrow the cuts are (high for sharp resonant material like drum overheads, low for general energy build-ups on channels, busses and masters), and selectivity is a threshold on which resonances are cut (high cuts only the most prominent, good for spot frequencies in string and wind instruments; low cuts almost all, to balance uneven material).

## Covers

What each main control means when Marco sets soothe2 on a take or a bus.

## Does not cover

Attack and release values; the soft and hard modes' numbers.
