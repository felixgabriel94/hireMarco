# Dynamic EQ in Pro-Q 3 moves a band's gain with the band's own level: compression or expansion of one frequency range

## Claim

A Bell or Shelf band in Pro-Q 3 can be made dynamic: its gain then changes with the level of a band-limited version of the input, by a dynamic range of -30 to +30 dB (negative is compression, positive expansion), with an automatic or custom threshold (soft knee) and program-dependent attack, release and knee; it is the surgical alternative to a multiband compressor. Static EQ generally works well; dynamic bands solve specific frequency problems such as a hi-hat too loud only on its hits or sibilance in a vocal, with a narrow dynamic bell.

## Covers

When a cut should happen only when the problem happens; what the dynamic controls do.

## Does not cover

Band shapes other than Bell and Shelf (they cannot be dynamic); linear-phase mode at the two highest resolutions, where dynamic EQ is not available.
