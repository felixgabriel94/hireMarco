# Pro-DS: threshold and range, the trigger band between 2 and 20 kHz, Single Vocal against Allround, wide band against split band

## Claim

Pro-DS triggers above a Threshold on a side-chain band set between 2 and 20 kHz (vocal esses usually around 8 to 10 kHz) and scales the reduction with Range; Single Vocal mode splits sibilance from non-sibilance on one vocal, Allround mode triggers on the band alone and is meant for whole mixes; Wide Band lowers the whole signal on an ess, Split Band only the highs, and split band with Allround serves in mastering as a high-frequency limiter.

## Covers

How the de-esser on the artist's vocal chain is set and when each mode is right.

## Does not cover

Threshold values, which the ess windows of the take decide.
