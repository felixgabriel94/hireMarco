# Mastering works on the stereo mixdown as the finished product; mixing balances the separate tracks

## Claim

Mixing sculpts and balances the separate tracks at the start of post-production; mastering takes the mix as one stereo file, puts the final touches on it, makes the record cohesive and prepares it for distribution formats, using only as many processes as needed.

## Covers

The division of labour between the two roles and the mastering engineer's object of work.

## Does not cover

The size of any move; the artist's own division between Marco and an outside mastering engineer.
