# Loudness meter targets and the loudness range reading in Pro-L 2

## Claim

Pro-L 2 offers target presets of -9 LUFS (described as common for audio CDs), -14 LUFS (roughly streaming services), -23 LUFS (EBU R128) and -24 LUFS (ATSC A/85); it implements BS.1770-4 with gated measurement, and shows loudness range (LRA) when the time scale is Integrated.

## Covers

What the limiter's own meter can show and the vendor's description of the targets.

## Does not cover

A target for the artist's own demo, which comes from his references.

## Sources

- FabFilter Pro-L 2 help, version 2.21 installed on this Mac, .../Resources/Html/using/loudnessmetering.html, read 2026-11-02: "The following common target levels are available as preset options: -9 LUFS (commonly used for audio CDs), -14 LUFS (roughly the target level used by streaming services like Spotify, iTunes or YouTube), -23 LUFS (the EBU R128 standard), or -24 LUFS (ATSC A/85 and TR-B32 standards)."; "If the time scale is set to Integrated, the loudness range (LRA) is also displayed to the left of the loudness meter. This is a measure for the variation of the loudness over time as defined by the EBU R128 standard."; "FabFilter Pro-L 2 implements ITU-R BS.1770-4 which also describes how to weigh surround channels properly."
