# Waves JJP Vocals: a type per voice, sensitivity set on the loudest section, a main section of dynamics and EQ with center faders as aux returns

## Claim

JJP Vocals offers three Types (Male, Male 2, Female) with JJP's defaults per type; the Sensitivity control is set on the loudest section of the track until the LED reads yellow (optimal), green is good, red very hot; the Main Section is the basic dynamics and EQ that all processing follows, the Center Section faders are auxiliary returns with their own processing fed pre-fader from the main section, each with a mute, and the Master fader is the output; the Clip LED lights at 0 dBFS.

## Covers

How the artist's vocal color box is leveled and what its two sections are.

## Does not cover

What each Main Section control (Lows, Highs, De-Esser and the rest) does in numbers: the guide installed on this Mac does not define them, so Marco keeps the artist's own settings on them and changes nothing there without his word.
