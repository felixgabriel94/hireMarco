# Pro-L 2's lookahead, attack, release and channel linking: the trade between loudness and cleanliness

## Claim

Short lookahead keeps transients and apparent loudness at the cost of possible distortion; long lookahead is safer and less loud. Short attack with long release is safer and cleaner but can pump and dull; long attack with short release is louder and more present at the cost of possible distortion. Transient channel linking is often best under 100 percent; release linking starts at 100 percent.

## Covers

How to reason about pumping, distortion and loudness on this limiter.

## Does not cover

Exact values, which are program-dependent and set on the bench.
