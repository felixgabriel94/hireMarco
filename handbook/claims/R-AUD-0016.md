# Pro-C 2: threshold, ratio, knee, range, the eight styles, attack and release, lookahead, hold, auto gain and parallel mix

## Claim

Pro-C 2 compresses above a Threshold by a Ratio (10:1 leaves one dB of every ten above the threshold; 1:1 none; infinity limits), rounds the knee from 0 to 72 dB, and caps the gain change with Range; its eight styles are Clean, Classic, Opto, Vocal (automatic knee and ratio, "compressing your lead vocal is as easy as choosing the right threshold"), Mastering (as transparent as possible), Bus (glue), Punch and Pumping; Attack runs 0.005 to 250 ms (fast for transient-rich material, too fast dulls or distorts other material), Release is program dependent with an Auto option, Lookahead preserves transients, Hold prolongs gain reduction, Auto Gain keeps the level while adjusting, and Dry Gain adds the uncompressed signal for parallel compression.

## Covers

What each control of the compressor on the artist's vocal chain does and which style suits which job.

## Does not cover

Values for a given take, which the chain reads and the bench proves.
