# A Pro-Q 3 band's solo plays the part of the spectrum that band affects

## Claim

In solo mode you do not hear the band's effect; you hear the part of the frequency spectrum the band affects, set by its frequency and Q. On a Low Cut or High Cut you hear what is being cut away.

## Covers

Why an engineer auditions a band before moving it. Marco does the same from the mix file, with the band's frequency and width, so a solo can never reach a print.

## Does not cover

Whether the slice is a problem: that is a judgment, heard and recorded per track.
