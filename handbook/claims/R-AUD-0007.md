# Pro-Q 3's nine shapes, slopes, Q, gain and frequency ranges, and note entry

## Claim

Pro-Q 3 offers Bell, Low Shelf, Low Cut, High Shelf, High Cut, Notch, Band Pass, Tilt Shelf and Flat Tilt; the slope runs 6 to 96 dB/octave on every shape (Brickwall on Low Cut and High Cut) so a Bell or Notch can be made ultra-narrow; frequency 5 Hz to 30 kHz, gain -30 to +30 dB (Bell and Shelf only), Q 1 is the default bandwidth and cannot be set at a 6 dB/octave slope; Gain-Q interaction narrows Q as gain rises (Bell only); a frequency can be typed as a note, "A4" or "C#2+13".

## Covers

Which shape does what, the ranges Marco may ask for, and that a note name is a valid frequency entry.

## Does not cover

When to choose a shape musically (situations); shelf Q values, which are internal.
