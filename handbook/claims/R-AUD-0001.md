# Loudness and true peak are measured by ITU-R BS.1770

## Claim

The ITU recommendation "Algorithms to measure audio programme loudness and true-peak audio level" is the measurement behind LUFS and dBTP; its current edition is BS.1770-5, approved November 2023 and in force.

## Covers

How loudness and true peak are defined for every meter Marco trusts (the audit, Pro-L 2, the platforms).

## Does not cover

The target levels themselves (R-AUD-0002, R-AUD-0003) and how any plug-in implements the meter.
