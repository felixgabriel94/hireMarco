# Pro-G: threshold, ratio and range of a gate or expander; the styles, with Vocal for breaths and bleed; side-chain filters

## Claim

Pro-G opens above a Threshold and expands below it by a Ratio (4:1 reduces each dB under the threshold toward 4 dB; past about 5:1 it acts as a gate), capped by Range (the floor); its styles are Classic, Clean, Vocal (opens gently on a breath, releases fast enough to cut noise or bleed), Guitar, Upward and Ducking; the side chain can be filtered with 48 dB per octave high- and low-pass filters.

## Covers

What the gate on the artist's vocal chain does between takes and how its floor is read.

## Does not cover

Values for a take, which the take's noise floor decides.
