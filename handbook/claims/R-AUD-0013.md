# Dither when reducing bit depth; truncation causes quantization distortion

## Claim

Reducing bit depth by truncation causes quantization distortion; dither (a little noise) avoids it, and noise shaping moves that noise to where the ear hears it less; Pro-L 2 dithers to 24, 22, 20, 18 or 16 bits or not at all.

## Covers

When a dither stage is needed (a 16-bit delivery) and why it is off for a 24-bit or float file.

## Does not cover

Which noise shaping curve to pick, a taste call per delivery.
