# The Manley Variable Mu is a variable-gain tube compressor made for the mix bus, with linked stereo and mid/side operation

## Claim

UA's Manley Variable Mu emulation is a dual-triode variable-gain compressor re-biased by its sidechain, transparent and smooth, whose purpose is the mix bus; linked stereo makes both channels compress the same amount so a one-sided transient does not shift the image; input and output matrices allow mid/side processing; Dual Input higher is more colored, lower more transparent; Threshold toward MIN lowers the threshold and increases compression; several continuous controls are unmarked (0 to 10) and meant to be set by ear.

## Covers

What the glue compressor in the artist's room is and how its controls read.

## Does not cover

Attack and recovery values; the hardware's ratio behaviour.
