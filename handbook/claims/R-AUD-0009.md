# Pro-L 2's eight limiting styles and what each suits

## Claim

Pro-L 2 has eight program-dependent styles, none better than another: Transparent (true to the source, rock and pop), Punchy (apparent, some pumping, single tracks and beat-driven music), Dynamic (transients enhanced before limiting), Allround (loudness and transparency balanced), Aggressive (near-clipping, EDM, rock, metal, pop), Modern (the default, all-purpose, very loud and clean at near-zero lookahead), Bus (glue, pump, squash; not transparent), Safe (no distortion, delicate material).

## Covers

Which style to name for a purpose and a genre.

## Does not cover

Lookahead, attack and release (R-AUD-0010).
