# Spotify normalises to -14 LUFS and asks for true peak under -1 dBTP, under -2 dBTP for louder masters

## Claim

Spotify plays tracks at -14 LUFS integrated by applying negative gain to louder masters and positive gain to softer ones; it asks for a true peak under -1 dBTP, and under -2 dBTP when the master is louder than -14 LUFS integrated; its Loud setting plays at -11 LUFS regardless of true peak.

## Covers

What happens to a master on Spotify at playback; the headroom Marco keeps on a streaming master.

## Does not cover

Other platforms, each of which is read from its own page before a delivery spec is promised; the artist's own loudness choice for a demo.

## Sources

- https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/loudness-normalization/, read 2026-11-02 (no date on the page): "We adjust tracks to -14 dB LUFS, according to the ITU 1770 standard."; "Loud: -11dB LUFS"; "We set this level regardless of maximum True Peak."; "Keep it below -1dB TP (True Peak) max."; "If your master is louder than -14dB integrated LUFS, keep True Peak below -2dB to avoid extra distortion."; "Negative gain is applied to louder masters so the loudness level is -14 dB LUFS."; "Positive gain is applied to softer masters so the loudness level is -14 dB LUFS."
