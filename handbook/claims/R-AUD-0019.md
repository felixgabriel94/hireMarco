# Auto-Tune Realtime X: key and scale set the target notes; Retune Speed in ms, Flex-Tune, Humanize and Detune

## Claim

Auto-Tune Realtime X tunes the audio to the notes that Key and Scale define together (Chromatic when the key is unknown); Retune Speed, in milliseconds, is how fast the correction is applied: 0 is the immediate "Auto-Tune Effect" that removes vibrato, 10 to 50 is natural, and larger values keep more vibrato and gesture but correct more slowly; Flex-Tune at 0 pulls every note to the scale and higher values correct only as the singer approaches the note; Humanize slows the retune only on sustained notes so short notes can be tuned fast without static long notes; Detune moves the A=440 reference by up to 100 cents.

## Covers

What each control on the artist's vocal chain does (Retune Speed 10 on the lead, 6 on BGVs, 12 on ad-libs, 8 on the robo; Detune 50, taste pass 2026-11-02) and how to choose them from the take.

## Does not cover

Formant and throat controls; the Advanced and Access editions, which have their own manuals.
