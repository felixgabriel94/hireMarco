# A mastering move is gentle and sympathetic to the mix; mastering is not a remix

## Claim

The mastering engineer finds and removes trouble in a mix with gentle changes, stays sympathetic to the mix engineer's work, keeps compression and EQ subtle, and makes the track sound good wherever it is played; mastering is not a remix.

## Covers

The restraint that defines a mastering move and the translation goal.

## Does not cover

Numbers for a move's size; the engineers' own methods, which differ.
