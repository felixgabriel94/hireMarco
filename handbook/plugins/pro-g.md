## How Marco reasons with it

- **Style**: Vocal on a voice (opens gently on a breath, closes fast enough
  for bleed); Clean on anything that must not flutter; Classic on drums;
  Ducking only for a voice-over job.
- **Threshold**: set between the take's noise floor and its quietest sung
  phrase, read from the take's level over time; a gate that clips the end of
  a phrase is set too high.
- **Ratio**: 2:1 to 5:1 is expansion, gentle on a voice; past about 5:1 it
  gates; the artist's 10:1 is a gate.
- **Range** (the floor): how far the noise drops; 20 dB hides room noise
  while keeping the take natural; 50 dB is silence between phrases.
- **Side chain filters** (48 dB per octave): narrow the trigger to the voice's
  band so a hat in the bleed does not open the gate.

## Verify

Breaths and phrase ends kept (the take's envelope before and after at the
phrase boundaries); the noise floor between phrases down by the range asked.
