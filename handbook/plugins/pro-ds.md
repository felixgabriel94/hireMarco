# FabFilter Pro-DS (version 1.21 installed; claim R-AUD-0017)

The de-esser on the artist's vocal chain (taste pass, 2026-11-02: threshold
-36 dB, range 6 dB, 7 to 14 kHz). From the help installed with the plug-in,
read 2026-11-02.

## How Marco reasons with it

- **Mode**: Single Vocal on one vocal take (it tells sibilance from the rest);
  Allround on a stack, a bus or a mix, where only the band decides.
- **Band** (2 to 20 kHz; esses usually 8 to 10 kHz): set from the measured
  ess frequency of the take (the per-take peak read), not from habit; the
  artist's own 7 to 14 kHz is his starting band.
- **Threshold**: set so the loudest ess triggers by 3 to 6 dB and ordinary
  consonants do not; the Audition Triggering button is how a human checks
  it, the ess windows of the per-take read are how Marco checks it.
- **Range**: the most reduction allowed; 4 to 6 dB on a vocal, more only when
  the take's esses measure more.
- **Wide Band** on a single vocal (the maker: often enough), **Split Band**
  on a mix or a complex source; split band with Allround is the mastering
  use, a high-frequency limiter, taken only when the audit names harshness.
- **Stereo Link** 100 percent unless one side carries the esses.

## Verify

The sibilance band and the hearing stack's sharpness before and after; the
non-ess windows unchanged.
