# FabFilter Pro-C 2 (version 2.17 installed; claim R-AUD-0016)

The compressor on every part of the artist's vocal chain (taste pass, 2026-11-02:
threshold -22.6 dB, 4.49:1, knee 18, attack 0.255 ms on the lead). From the
help installed with the plug-in, read 2026-11-02.

## How Marco reasons with it

- **Style**: Vocal on a lead or a double (automatic knee and ratio; the
  threshold is the one decision); Clean when the compression must not be
  heard; Opto for a slow, soft hold; Bus for glue on a stack or the mix bus;
  Mastering only on a master, and only when the bench shows it earned
  something; Punch and Pumping are effects, chosen on purpose.
- **Threshold** (dB): set so the loudest phrases take the gain reduction
  the part needs (2 to 4 dB on a lead that must sit still, 6 or more when the
  take is wild), read on the gain reduction meter, never guessed.
- **Ratio**: 2:1 to 4:1 on a vocal; higher turns level control into limiting.
- **Knee** (0 to 72 dB): soft (18 to 30) for transparency on a voice; hard
  for a drum.
- **Attack** (0.005 to 250 ms): fast catches transients but can dull or
  distort a voice; a vocal usually wants 1 to 10 ms unless the plosives
  must be caught.
- **Release**: program dependent; Auto on for a voice unless pumping shows
  on the bench; then slower by hand.
- **Lookahead** and **Hold**: lookahead for transparent peaks; hold for a
  pumping feel, not on a vocal by default.
- **Auto Gain** on while setting, off before the bench so the comparison is
  loudness-matched by the verifier, not by the plug-in; **Dry Gain** for
  parallel body when the take loses life under compression.
- **Stereo Link**: 100 percent on a stereo bus unless one side needs its
  own control; mid-only or side-only only for a named stereo problem.

## Verify

The take's crest and short-term range before and after; no pumping at the
beat rate on the bench; the vocal's level against the rest unchanged after
make-up.
